Kontrol Features

A comprehensive overview of the Kontrol Estate Management Ecosystem designed for security, efficiency, and community.

Admin Module (Estate Management)

Resident Management

  • Approval Portal: Structured workflow for reviewing and managing resident applications.
  • Secure Invitations: Generate unique, time-limited links for direct resident onboarding.
  • Resident Directory: Full database with status tracking and household details.

Financial Management

  • Automated Invoicing: Periodic billing based on resident count and chosen tiers.
  • Integrated Payments: Secure payment processing via Paystack gateway.
  • Financial Audits: Real-time tracking of successful and pending transactions.

Communication Hub

  • Estate Board: Broadcast announcements with media support to all residents.
  • Moderation: Manage resident engagement and community discussions.
  • Staff Management: Registry for gatehouse personnel and security monitors.

Resident Module (App Experience)

Visitor Management

  • Access Codes: Generate secure 6-digit codes for guest entry.
  • Flexible Access: Support for one-time guest codes or recurring visitor access.
  • Visit History: Real-time feed showing visitor entry and exit times.

Community Engagement

  • Interactive Notices: View estate news and participate in community discussions.
  • Emergency Contacts: Quick access to management and local emergency services.
  • Household Control: Manage family members and dependents under one account.

Advanced Connectivity

  • Telegram Integration: Generate codes and receive alerts via Telegram.
  • Real-time Notifications: Instant push alerts for guest arrivals and security events.

Security Module (Gatehouse)

Validation & Entry

  • Code Scanner: Instantly verify visitor access codes at the gate.
  • Digital Logs: Automated time-stamped entry/exit logs replacing paper books.
  • Situational Awareness: Live rolling feed of expected and active visitors.

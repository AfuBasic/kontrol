### Approved Product & Design Specification

---

# IMPORTANT

This document is a product specification.

It is NOT a brainstorming document.

It is NOT a design inspiration document.

It is NOT a suggestion.

All requirements contained in this specification are considered approved requirements.

Do not redesign the experience.

Do not introduce additional pages.

Do not introduce additional navigation items.

Do not invent additional feature sections.

Do not expose product capabilities beyond what is specified.

Deviation is not permitted unless explicitly required for usability, accessibility, responsiveness, or technical implementation.

---

# PROJECT OVERVIEW

Kontrol is a modern estate operations platform.

It helps residential communities manage:

- Visitors
- Estate Dues
- Announcements
- Resident Complaints
- Security Coordination

The public website exists to:

- Build trust
- Explain the core value of Kontrol
- Generate estate applications
- Encourage communities to start a free trial

The website should not function as a complete product catalog.

The website should intentionally reveal only a portion of the product.

---

# TARGET AUDIENCE

Primary Audience:

- Estate Chairmen
- Estate Secretaries
- Estate Managers
- Facility Managers
- Property Developers
- Community Executives

The audience is operational.

They are not technical buyers.

They are not software engineers.

They care about:

- Simplicity
- Reliability
- Ease of use
- Organization
- Communication
- Accountability

---

# FEATURE DISCLOSURE POLICY

This section is mandatory.

The public website should intentionally hide advanced product functionality.

Only publicly showcase:

- Visitors
- Payments
- Announcements
- Resident Complaints

Do NOT publicly showcase:

- Property Owner Management
- Household Management
- Internal Estate Operations
- Advanced Permissions
- Estate Staff Workflows
- Future Product Modules
- Administrative Automation
- Internal Operational Features

The goal is to create confidence and curiosity, not expose the entire product roadmap.

# THEME SUPPORT

Light Mode and Dark Mode are mandatory.

Dark Mode is not an afterthought.

Both themes must feel intentionally designed.

Theme selection must persist.

Theme switching should feel smooth and polished.

---

# ESTATE IMAGERY

Estate imagery is a core part of the brand.

Light Mode:

- Daytime estate
- Natural lighting
- Visible greenery
- Premium residential environment

Dark Mode:

- Same estate concept
- Evening atmosphere
- Warm building lights
- Premium residential environment

Do not use:

- Office buildings
- Generic skylines
- Abstract illustrations

The imagery must clearly communicate residential communities.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Font Family:

Inter

Headlines:

- Bold
- Clean
- Confident

Body:

- Highly readable
- Human
- Simple

Avoid excessive font weights.

Avoid excessive letter spacing.

---

# SPACING

Whitespace is a feature.

The layout should breathe.

Avoid:

- Dense layouts
- Crowded sections
- Information overload

Each section should communicate one clear idea.

---

# MOTION SYSTEM

Motion is mandatory.

The website should feel alive.

The website should never feel static.

Motion must be subtle.

Motion must never feel distracting.

---

# SCROLL REVEALS

Every major section should animate into view.

Examples:

- Fade Up
- Fade In
- Slide Up
- Small Scale Reveal

Use staggered reveals where appropriate.

---

# HERO MOTION

Floating activity cards should have subtle movement.

Examples:

Visitor Arrived

Announcement Sent

Complaint Acknowledged

Estate Due Paid

Movement should feel slow and natural.

Think breathing.

Not floating balloons.

---

# SCREENSHOT MOTION

Product screenshots should animate into view.

Alternate:

- Left
- Right
- Fade Up

Create rhythm throughout the page.

---

# CARD INTERACTIONS

Cards should include subtle hover effects.

Examples:

- Slight Lift
- Soft Shadow
- Border Highlight

Avoid dramatic animations.

---

# TIMELINE MOTION

The timeline section should progressively reveal events as users scroll.

The story should unfold naturally.

---

# THEME TRANSITIONS

Theme switching should be smooth.

No flashing.

No abrupt color changes.

---

# PERFORMANCE

Use GPU-friendly animations.

Prefer:

- opacity
- transform
- translate
- scale

Avoid expensive layout calculations.

Maintain excellent performance.

---

# ACCESSIBILITY

Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Reduce non-essential animations.

Maintain usability.
